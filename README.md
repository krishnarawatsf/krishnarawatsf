## About Me

Final year **B.Tech Cybersecurity** student specializing in **Cybersecurity GRC, Cyber Risk & Compliance, and Cyber Strategy & Governance**. I build enterprise-grade governance frameworks, risk assessment methodologies, and security control audit toolkits that translate complex technical gaps into executive-actionable remediation roadmaps.

**Core Focus Areas:**
- **Cybersecurity GRC** — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control mapping, gap assessments, and ISMS readiness
- **Cyber Risk Assessment** — Quantitative risk scoring (3×3 matrices), risk registers, and remediation tracking
- **AI Governance** — Algorithmic risk classification, bias mitigation frameworks, and responsible AI lifecycle models
- **Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)** — Weighted vendor scoring, supply chain due diligence, and continuous monitoring
- **Regulatory Compliance** — RBI Cybersecurity Directions, DPDP Act 2023, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001
